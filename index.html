സ്നേഹസ്പർശമായി PTH - സഹചാരി

പുതിയ മാളിയേക്കൽ സയ്യിദ് അഹമ്മദ് പൂക്കോയ തങ്ങളുടെ സേവനപാരമ്പര്യവും മാനുഷിക ദർശനവും ഉൾക്കൊണ്ട് iuml നേതൃത്വത്തിൽ പ്രവർത്തിക്കുന്ന PTH (പൂക്കോയ തങ്ങൾ ഹോസ്പിസ്), പാലിയേറ്റീവ് കെയർ രംഗത്ത് സ്നേഹത്തിന്റെയും കരുതലിന്റെയും മാതൃകയാണ്. രോഗവും പ്രയാസങ്ങളും നേരിടുന്നവർക്ക് ആശ്വാസവും പരിചരണവും നൽകുക എന്ന ലക്ഷ്യത്തോടെ skssf സഹചാരി പ്രവർത്തിക്കുന്നു.

മേഖലയിലെ ആവശ്യക്കാരായ രോഗികൾക്ക് സൗജന്യ പാലിയേറ്റീവ് പരിചരണവും ഹോംകെയർ സേവനങ്ങളും നൽകി, രോഗികൾക്കും കുടുംബങ്ങൾക്കും ശാരീരികവും മാനസികവുമായ പിന്തുണ ഉറപ്പാക്കുകയാണ് മുഴക്കുന്ന് PTH-സഹചാരി. സാമ്പത്തിക പരിമിതികൾ പരിചരണത്തിന് തടസ്സമാകരുതെന്ന ഉറച്ച വിശ്വാസത്തോടെ, കരുതലിന്റെ സാന്നിധ്യമായി ഓരോ കുടുംബത്തിനൊപ്പവും സഹചാരി റിലീഫ് സെൽ മുഴക്കുന്ന് നിലകൊള്ളുന്നു.

01 Critical Relief

Pain Management

Specialized palliative evaluation and multi-modal pain alleviation protocols administered by experienced physicians, ensuring cancer and chronic disease patients live free from excruciating physical distress.

  • Scientific WHO analgesic ladder titration & monitoring
  • Opioid, analgesic & adjuvant therapeutic protocols
  • Breakthrough pain relief & bedside solace therapy
02 Tissue Care

Bedsore Management

Comprehensive wound treatment and prevention protocols for pressure ulcers in immobilized bedridden patients, promoting rapid tissue recovery while safeguarding against painful systemic infections.

  • Stage I through IV pressure ulcer debridement & dressing
  • Free distribution of specialized ripple air beds & water beds
  • Caregiver repositioning schedules & barrier protection training
03 Bedside Nursing

Catheterisation

Aseptic insertion, hygienic maintenance, and regular timely replacement of urinary catheters directly at patient bedsides by certified palliative care nurses to prevent urinary tract infections and trauma.

  • Sterile Foley and silicone catheter bedside replacement
  • Bladder irrigation, obstruction clearing & infection prevention
  • Free uro-bags, drainage kits & caregiver hygiene orientation
04 Stoma Care

Colostomy Care

Sensitive, expert ostomy care offering stoma health evaluations, leak-proof appliance fitting, peristomal skin complication treatment, and compassionate lifestyle reassurance for patients and family bystanders.

  • Precise stoma appliance fitting & barrier paste application
  • Free provision of quality colostomy bags & accessories
  • Peristomal skin excoriation management & comfort guidance
05 Solace & Peace

EOL Care (End of Life Care)

Holistic, dignified, round-the-clock supportive care during the final stages of life, ensuring profound physical comfort, freedom from breathing distress, serenity, and gentle emotional and spiritual accompaniment.

  • Continuous terminal symptom management & pain freedom
  • Peaceful hospice inpatient sanctuary & home bedside support
  • Family bereavement solace & moral reassurance
06 Daily Care

General Bedside Care

Comprehensive day-to-day nursing assistance, vital signs surveillance, personal hygiene support, and tender bedside companionship for elderly, frail, and completely bedridden patients.

  • Regular blood pressure, pulse, glucose & oxygen level checks
  • Bed-sponge baths, oral hygiene & skin hydration therapy
  • Bystander respite assistance & compassionate home visits
07 Fluid Therapy

Lymphoedema Management

Specialized therapeutic care to alleviate severe and painful limb swelling resulting from cancer therapies, lymph node dissections, or chronic lymphatic blockages, restoring mobility and skin health.

  • Certified Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD) gentle massage
  • Multi-layer therapeutic compression bandaging application
  • Skin integrity preservation & cellulitis prevention routines
08 100% Free Pharmacy

Medicine Support

Free monthly procurement and doorstep distribution of crucial palliative pharmaceuticals, pain medications, and life-supporting drugs to ensure continuous therapy without financial barriers.

  • 100% free prescription palliative medications & analgesics
  • Physician medication review & dosage compliance tracking
  • Prompt doorstep delivery during home care visits
09 Clinical Procedure

Paracentesis

Skilled bedside abdominal paracentesis performed by palliative medical officers to safely drain recurrent ascites fluid, bringing rapid relief from severe abdominal distension, nausea, and shortness of breath.

  • Sterile abdominal tap & controlled fluid drainage at bedside
  • Immediate abdominal pressure relief & respiratory comfort
  • Post-procedure vital monitoring & nursing follow-up
10 Restorative Care

Rehabilitation & Mobility

Gentle bedside physiotherapy, passive range-of-motion therapy, and assistive equipment support designed to preserve physical autonomy, reduce stiffness, and restore personal dignity for recovering or chronic patients.

  • Bedside physical therapy & joint contracture prevention
  • Free loaning of wheelchairs, walkers & commode chairs
  • Safe transfer training & ergonomic caregiver guidance
11 Enteral Feeding

Ryle's Tube Insertion

Safe nasogastric (NG) tube insertion, verified anatomical placement, and regular sterile replacement by clinical specialists to ensure patients with severe swallowing disorders receive necessary nutrition and hydration.

  • Aseptic NG tube insertion & auscultatory position check
  • Prevention of aspiration pneumonia & tube obstruction
  • Family orientation on enteral diet preparation & hygiene
12 Airway Management

Tracheostomy Care

Expert respiratory airway maintenance, gentle bronchial suctioning, inner tube cannula cleaning, and peristomal skin dressing to safeguard patients with artificial breathing tubes against respiratory emergencies.

  • Sterile airway suctioning & secretion clearance
  • Inner cannula sanitization, dressing & tie renewals
  • 24x7 emergency helpline access & caregiver confidence training
13 Advanced Dressing

Wound Care

Advanced clinical wound dressings for malignant fungating lesions, chronic diabetic foot ulcers, gangrene, and complicated surgical wounds using modern antiseptic and odor-control therapies.

  • Modern silver, hydrogel & charcoal dressing applications
  • Malignant ulcer exudate & odor management with pain-free dressing
  • Infection control, tissue viability evaluation & continuous tracking
14 Mobility Aid

Walking Stick

Free distribution of sturdy, ergonomic, height-adjustable walking sticks and quad canes to provide vital balance, gait confidence, and fall prevention for recovering and elderly palliative patients.

  • Ergonomic grip with anti-skid high-traction rubber ferrules
  • Lightweight aluminum height-adjustable construction
  • Doorstep sizing, patient gait guidance & volunteer support
15 Bedsore Relief

Air Bed

Medical-grade alternating pressure ripple air mattresses provided to continuously redistribute body weight, enhance capillary blood circulation, and prevent agonizing pressure ulcers in bedridden patients.

  • Ultra-quiet medical compressor pump with continuous ripple cycle
  • Alternating tubular air cell inflation for maximum pressure relief
  • Free home delivery, setup & maintenance replacement service
16 Wheelchair Solace

Wheelchair

Complimentary loaning of high-durability, foldable wheelchairs equipped with safety brakes, cushioned backrests, and swing-away leg rests, restoring vital locomotion and dignity for immobilized individuals.

  • Compact foldable frame with heavy-duty puncture-resistant wheels
  • Dual attendant handbrakes, safety belt & padded armrests
  • Bedside delivery, caregiver operation training & long-term maintenance
17 Clinical Bed

Hospital Bed

Multi-function adjustable clinical hospital beds provided free of cost to homecare patients, allowing effortless elevation of back and legs for improved breathing, dignified feeding, and safe nursing care.

  • Smooth dual-crank Fowler mechanism for head and foot elevation
  • Collapsible aluminum side safety rails to prevent accidental falls
  • Complete doorstep transport, room assembly & waterproof medical mattress
18 Respiratory Care

Nebulizer Machine

Heavy-duty medical aerosol compressor nebulizers supplied to patients suffering from chronic asthma, COPD, bronchitis, and respiratory congestion for prompt, direct bronchodilator delivery at home.

  • High-efficiency particle atomization for rapid bronchial relief
  • Complete sanitized kit with adult/child masks, chamber & tubing
  • Comprehensive caregiver orientation on aseptic usage and cleaning
19 Gait Support

Walker

Sturdy, lightweight four-point reciprocal and rigid aluminum mobility walkers distributed to assist post-operative, neurological, and elderly patients in re-establishing walking balance without fear of falling.

  • Sturdy anodized aluminum frame with convenient one-touch folding
  • Soft-grip contoured handles and height-adjustable slip-resistant legs
  • Free home delivery, balance assessment & safe gait coaching
20 Cooling Comfort

Water Bed

Specially designed medical water beds providing buoyant, zero-pressure point support and cooling temperature regulation for terminal cancer patients, severe burn victims, and chronic bedridden individuals.

  • Hydro-buoyant weight distribution preventing painful skin friction
  • High-grade puncture-resistant medical vinyl with antimicrobial surface
  • On-site home filling, leak verification & hygienic maintenance support
21 Life Support

Oxygen Concentrator

Continuous, clinical-grade medical oxygen concentrators provided cost-free to advanced cancer, severe respiratory failure, and palliative homecare patients requiring continuous oxygen support.

  • Pure 93% ± 3% medical oxygen delivery up to 5L / 10L per minute flow
  • Equipped with sterile nasal cannulas, humidifier bottles & backup sensors
  • 24x7 emergency helpline backup, rapid dispatch & voltage regulator setup
22 Sanitary Dignity

Commode Chair

Sanitary bedside commode chairs and toilet elevation aids provided to preserve patient privacy, cleanliness, and independence when traveling to the bathroom is painful or physically dangerous.

  • Rust-proof reinforced frame with removable sanitary bucket and splash lid
  • Height-adjustable non-skid rubber feet & cushioned waterproof seating
  • Direct doorstep delivery, hygienic care supplies & caregiver guidance

Extending Compassion & Relief to Wayanad

In the wake of the catastrophic landslides that struck Wayanad, the dedicated volunteer cadre of SKSSF Viqaya and Sahachari Relief Cell Muzhakkunnu swiftly mobilized to ground zero. HELP & COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our 100% free palliative homecare, medical equipment lending, emergency helpline, and community relief in Muzhakkunnu.

Is palliative care & medicine completely free?

Yes — 100% free of charge. PTH Sahachari provides all doctor home visits, nursing care, pain relief treatments, essential medicines, and wound dressings with zero billing, regardless of background or financial status.

What medical equipment is available for home care?

We provide hospital fowler beds, oxygen concentrators, air and water ripple mattresses, wheelchairs, suction apparatus, and walking aids directly to patients’ homes on a free loan basis for as long as medically needed.

Who is eligible to receive care from Sahachari?

Any patient suffering from bedridden chronic conditions, advanced cancer, severe stroke, terminal ailments, or debilitating age-related health crises residing within our service territory is fully eligible.

How long until a medical home care team arrives?

For new registrations, our assessment team schedules home visits within 24 to 48 hours. In acute pain emergencies or critical respiratory distress, our 24/7 care helpline prioritizes prompt same-day emergency assistance.

How can I donate or support Sahachari Relief Cell?

Sahachari is 100% community-funded. You can support by scanning our UPI QR code, transferring directly to South Indian Bank (A/C: 0782073000000350, IFSC: SIBL0000782), sponsoring monthly patient kits, or volunteering your time with our field teams.

Which areas & panchayats does the mobile unit serve?

Based near Juma Masjid, Muzhakkunnu P.O., our mobile palliative care team regularly visits homes across Muzhakkunnu, Thillankeri, Peravoor, Iritty, and nearby rural hamlets throughout Kannur district.
