ABOUT US

Pookoya Thangal Hospice

The Indian Union Muslim League is a political movement that has been at the forefront of unparalleled humanitarian and charitable initiatives in the field of healthcare and social welfare.

Inspired by the enduring legacy of P.M.S.A. Pookoya Thangal, PTH Hospice provides completely cost-free palliative care, dedicated bedside visits, essential medical equipment, and human solace to patients and families in need.

OUR SERVICES & COMMITMENT

Comprehensive Care Wrapped with Compassion

At Pookoya Thangal Hospice, every service is rendered 100% free of charge, bringing medical expertise, personal dignity, and heartfelt solace straight to the doorsteps of patients and families.

01 Core Mission

Home Care Visits

Specialized mobile medical teams consisting of doctors, certified palliative nurses, and dedicated caregivers visit bedridden and terminally ill patients directly in their homes.

  • Regular home medical consultations
  • Catheter, wound & bed-sore dressing
  • Systematic pain relief & comfort therapy
02 In-Patient

Hospice Care Center

A peaceful, welcoming sanctuary providing round-the-clock supportive in-patient care for patients who require acute symptom management, continuous nursing, and comfort care.

  • 24/7 Doctor & nurse supervision
  • Serene, hygienic & dignified rooms
  • Warm bystander hospitality & care
03 Direct Aid

Medicines & Medical Gear

Providing vital palliative medications and loaning costly critical medical appliances without any deposit or charges, keeping patient care continuous without financial hardship.

  • Oxygen concentrators & cylinders loan
  • Specialized air beds, water beds & wheelchairs
  • Monthly free life-saving medicine support
04 Solace

Psychosocial & Family Counseling

Prolonged illness affects the whole family emotionally. Our trained counselors offer compassionate guidance, mental reassurance, and bereavement solace.

  • Mental relief & moral encouragement
  • Bystander burnout mitigation
  • Empathetic bereavement follow-ups
05 Welfare

Sahachari Rehabilitation Aid

Extending support beyond medicine through monthly food grocery distribution and educational assistance for school-going children of impoverished bedridden patients.

  • Nutritious grocery food kits
  • Children's education scholarships
  • Community volunteer care network

ONE DAY MEGA HOMECARE DRIVE

Standing With Wayanad,
Beyond The Disaster

Following the catastrophic Wayanad landslide disaster, Pookoya Thangal Hospice mobilized an emergency response cadre to conduct a comprehensive One-Day Mega Homecare Drive. Our multidisciplinary medical teams reached into cut-off hill terrains to assess and address the urgent medical, social, livelihood, and psychosocial recovery needs of affected families.

COMMUNITY CARE FORCE

Trained Volunteers: The Soul of Sahachari

Beyond medical charts and clinical therapy, genuine palliative solace begins with empathetic human companionship. With 5,839 certified community volunteers and 954 dedicated student caregivers, our grassroots movement brings solace, warmth, and dignity directly to bedridden patients across Kerala.

118 Certified Training Modules

Volunteers undergo comprehensive clinical and psychosocial orientation on patient hygiene, bed-sore dressing, pain observation, and empathetic bedside communication.

Psychosocial & Family Reassurance

Offering compassionate listening, bystander burnout relief, and emotional comfort so that no family feels isolated or helpless during terminal illness.

954 Student Volunteer Movement

Mobilizing university and school students through specialized youth sessions, cultivating lifelong empathy, social responsibility, and selfless community leadership.
